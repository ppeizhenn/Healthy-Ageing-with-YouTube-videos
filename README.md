# Healthy-Ageing-with-YouTube-videos
Document Clustering + Topic Modelling on YouTube videos (subtitles)

<ABSTRACT>
  
As populations age worldwide, it is vital to “add life to years,” enabling older adults to maintain independence and quality of life. In support of this goal, the United Nations declared 2021–2030 the Decade of Healthy Ageing, promoting action across five pillars: Physical Activity, Cognitive Activity, Good Nutrition, Social Engagement, and Mental Health. To strengthen policies and programs design, governments are exploring leveraging YouTube as a scalable, accessible medium for Healthy Ageing education. Healthy Ageing–related YouTube videos were collected and analysed to (i) estimate the distribution of content across the five pillars using soft clustering, and (ii) apply LDA topic modelling to characterise pillar-specific themes. These analyses assess whether the platform’s content is sufficiently reliable to help educate the ageing community, thereby allowing governments to focus on less-informed pillars during policy planning.

Across nine model variants, the best-performing representation - word2vec_CBOW paired with Gaussian Mixture Models revealed that video contents are concentrated in Physical Activity, Cognitive Activity, and Good Nutrition, with notable cross-pillar overlap on these three pillars within individual videos. Targeted manual review on topic labels identified indicate generally consistent, relevant themes in these three pillars that are safe and legal, supporting their potential use as education materials for older adults. By contrast, Mental Health and Social Engagement were comparatively underrepresented, highlighting actionable gaps for targeted policy development and outreach by governments.
